About APSDI

Who we are.

APSDI is a non-profit NGO governed by Decree-Law No. 004/2001, operating in the Democratic Republic of Congo since 2022. Headquartered in Bukavu, South Kivu, it has a mandate to operate throughout the entire country.

Established 2022  ·  Headquartered in Bukavu  ·  Nationwide mandate

Vision & Mission

Dignity and lasting resilience for every community.

Our Vision

A future where integrated communities throughout the Democratic Republic of Congo enjoy their human dignity and sustainable resilience.

Our Mission

APSDI drives development through an integrated approach that links environmental protection, food security, public health promotion and social protection with peace promotion, grounded in gender mainstreaming.

Overall Objective

To enhance the socio-economic resilience of conflict-affected populations in the Democratic Republic of Congo by fostering peaceful coexistence, protecting natural resources, improving health outcomes, strengthening food security and mainstreaming gender within community development programs.

Institutional Identity

Seven principles define who we are.

They shape how APSDI positions itself and how it works alongside communities and partners.

Integrity & Apolitical Engagement

Equity & Social Justice

Human Dignity & Resilience

Moving communities from vulnerability to sustainable resilience.

Community Participation

Fostering inclusive, community-led development to ensure local ownership.

Thematic Areas

Eight areas of intervention, each with clear expected results.

Every thematic area pairs concrete interventions with the results APSDI commits to achieving.

1. Peace Promotion & Social Cohesion

Key interventions

Promote dialogue, mediation and community peacebuilding among host communities, IDPs, refugees and returnees; strengthen the capacity of local leaders, women, youth and CSOs in conflict prevention and resolution.

Expected results

Community peace committees strengthened; reduced conflicts over land, natural resources and services; increased participation of women and youth in peace processes.

2. Environmental Protection & Climate Resilience

Key interventions

Promote sustainable natural resource management, climate-smart agriculture, restoration of community forests, improved waste management and alternative energy solutions.

Expected results

Increased tree planting and forest restoration; improved climate-smart practices and waste management; reduced deforestation and greater environmental awareness.

3. Health Promotion

Key interventions

Strengthen preventive and community-based health services, focusing on maternal and child health, nutrition, sexual and reproductive health, hygiene, sanitation and disease prevention.

Expected results

Increased immunisation and health awareness; reduced preventable diseases; improved hygiene and sanitation; strengthened community health worker capacity.

4. Food Security & Nutrition

Key interventions

Improve sustainable crop and livestock production, post-harvest handling and storage, household income diversification and nutrition education.

Expected results

Increased agricultural productivity; diversified household incomes; improved maternal and child nutrition; reduced food insecurity among vulnerable households.

5. Gender Equality & Women’s Empowerment

Key interventions

Promote women’s participation in leadership, decision-making and economic activities, while strengthening prevention of and response to gender-based violence and male engagement.

Expected results

Increased women’s participation in leadership; improved access to livelihoods; stronger GBV prevention and response; increased male support for gender equality.

6. Socio-economic Development & Livelihoods

Key interventions

Provide vocational and entrepreneurship training, strengthen financial inclusion, support MSMEs and savings groups, and improve access to markets and livelihood opportunities.

Expected results

Youth and women acquire marketable skills; businesses established or strengthened; savings and loan groups expanded; increased household income and economic resilience.

7. Youth Empowerment

Key interventions

Increase youth participation in peacebuilding, entrepreneurship, civic engagement, employment and environmental conservation through skills development and youth-led initiatives.

Expected results

Youth-led businesses established; youth peace ambassadors trained; increased employment and civic participation; greater youth engagement in environmental protection.

8. Community Governance

Key interventions

Strengthen community development committees, accountability mechanisms, citizen participation and collaboration between communities and local authorities in development planning.

Expected results

Functional community development structures; greater transparency and accountability; increased citizen participation; improved collaboration between communities and local authorities.

What We Stand For

Ten values guide every program.

They shape how staff engage communities, how partners are chosen, and how progress is measured.

Peace & Social Cohesion

Promoting dialogue, reconciliation, non-violence, conflict prevention and peaceful coexistence among communities.

Human Dignity & Human Rights

Respecting the rights, dignity and equal worth of every person without discrimination.

Gender Equality & Social Inclusion

Ensuring equal opportunities, participation and benefits for women, men, youth, persons with disabilities and marginalised groups.

Community Ownership & Participation

Empowering communities to lead decision-making, planning, implementation and monitoring of development activities.

Environmental Stewardship

Protecting natural resources and promoting climate resilience, biodiversity conservation and sustainable environmental practices.

Integrity, Transparency & Accountability

Managing resources ethically, transparently and responsibly while remaining accountable to beneficiaries, partners and donors.

Resilience & Sustainable Development

Strengthening individual and institutional capacity to withstand shocks and sustain development gains beyond the project period.

Partnership & Collaboration

Working closely with government institutions, civil society, UN agencies, communities and the private sector to achieve shared goals.

Innovation & Learning

Promoting creative solutions, evidence-based programming, continuous learning and adaptive management.

Equity & Justice

Ensuring fair access to services, opportunities, resources and justice, particularly for vulnerable and marginalised populations.

Governance

Built on transparency and shared oversight.

Transparency is built into our statutory structure: a General Assembly, a Board of Directors and a Supervisory Board responsible for administrative and financial oversight.

General Assembly

The organisation's members convene to set overall direction and provide strategic oversight.

Board of Directors

Guides strategy and decision-making between assembly sessions.

Supervisory Board

Responsible for administrative and financial oversight, ensuring transparency and accountability.

Building resilience requires partnership.

We welcome partnerships with donors, government institutions, UN agencies, civil society and the private sector to design and deliver programs that last across the Democratic Republic of Congo.
