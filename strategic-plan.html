Strategic Plan 2026–2030

Strategic pillars and interventions.

APSDI’s strategy for 2026–2030 is built around one general objective and eight strategic objectives, aligned with global, continental and national development frameworks.

Policy Alignment

Aligned with global, continental and national frameworks.

UN Sustainable Development Goals

Contributing directly to SDG 1 (No Poverty), SDG 2 (Zero Hunger), SDG 3 (Good Health) and SDG 5 (Gender Equality) through multisectoral field programs.

African Union Agenda 2063

Supporting the vision of an integrated, prosperous and peaceful Africa through cross-border dialogue, Great Lakes mediation and digital literacy.

DRC Vision 2050

Supporting the national transition to a middle-income economy by transforming small-scale agriculture into commercial SME networks.

PNSD 2024–2028

Advancing the National Strategic Development Plan’s priorities: human capital development, peace consolidation and economic diversification.

Strategic Objectives 2026–2030

One general objective, eight strategic objectives.

General Objective

Sustainable strengthening of the resilience, health, peace and socio-economic development of vulnerable communities.

Infection Reduction

Decrease susceptibility to HIV and communicable diseases.

Protection Services

Multidisciplinary medical, legal and psychosocial support for GBV survivors.

Peace & Cohesion

Local mediation and cross-border reconciliation.

Food & Nutrition

Technical agricultural support and post-harvest management.

WASH Access

Safe drinking water, hygiene and sanitation.

Climate Resilience

Reforestation and natural disaster risk reduction.

Economic Empowerment

SMEs, community savings, and job creation for youth and women.

Digital Transformation

Integrating sustainable technologies into agriculture and community services.
