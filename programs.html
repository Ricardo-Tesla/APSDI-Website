Our Programs

Peace & Governance

Social cohesion, protection and regional integration.

Social Protection & GBV Response

We provide multidisciplinary support encompassing medical, psychosocial, legal and financial assistance for survivors of gender-based violence (GBV). This intervention specifically targets the most marginalised groups, including internally displaced persons (IDPs), refugees and people with disabilities, ensuring their safety and reintegration into society.

Peace, Culture, Social Cohesion & Regional Integration (PCSCRI)

The program is designed to achieve sustainable peace and stability through regional cooperation and coordination. It addresses transnational security threats by strengthening regional organisations and providing tools for nonviolent engagement in conflict.

Focused on women and youth, PCSCRI uses workshops, discussion groups and films to share the power of nonviolence and foster social cohesion within the Great Lakes region. This reinforces APSDI’s statutory mission to transform regional diversity into a source of strength through dialogue, mediation and the protection of human rights.

Health & Nutrition

Community health, food security and WASH.

Health

APSDI implements multisectoral health interventions to reduce communities’ vulnerability to pests and infectious diseases through intensified prevention and awareness campaigns. By deploying community health workers, the organisation strengthens referral and counter-referral systems to professional healthcare structures.

APSDI also provides essential medical and psychosocial assistance to vulnerable groups and survivors of gender-based violence.

Food Security & Nutrition-Sensitive Agriculture (FSNSA)

The program targets the unacceptably high levels of malnutrition in rural areas, specifically stunting in children. APSDI uses it to build capacity in nutrition-sensitive agriculture through dietary diversity and the promotion of nutritious food intake.

By focusing on behaviour change and strengthening the capacity of value chain actors, FSNSA supports Farmer Field Schools and livestock management. A critical focus is combating chronic malnutrition in children under five and providing nutritional support to pregnant and breastfeeding women through a network of community health workers.

This approach transforms small-scale producers into commercial actors while ensuring that children under five and breastfeeding women receive the nutritional support they need to move beyond chronic vulnerability.

Water, Sanitation & Environmental Health (WSEH)

The program addresses safe drinking water and sanitation as fundamental drivers of community health. Integrated into APSDI’s broader health work, WSEH engages in research and development for watershed management and water harvesting.

It runs literacy campaigns and environmental and sanitation education to combat environment-related diseases and waterborne infections, in line with APSDI’s statutory goal of improving access to drinking water and strengthening hygiene for vulnerable groups such as war-displaced persons and refugees in conflict areas.

Environment & Livelihoods

Natural resources, SMEs and digital inclusion.

Natural Resources & Environment Management (NREM)

The program is a cornerstone of APSDI’s commitment to environmental stewardship and climate resilience. It offers innovative project development, research and technical assistance focused on the efficient use and conservation of bio-resources. Through capacity building and policy reviews on climate change and biodiversity, it carries out APSDI’s mandate to protect the sensitive ecosystems of the lake and river basins.

NREM also promotes renewable energy and sustainable technologies, supporting APSDI’s strategic goal of contributing to a circular economy and reducing the risk of natural disasters through awareness and demonstration programs. Interventions include watershed reforestation, soil conservation and disaster risk reduction to help communities adapt to climate change.

Economic Empowerment & SME Support (EESS)

APSDI drives job creation for youth and women by facilitating the establishment of small and medium enterprises (SMEs) and agricultural cooperatives. Our bottom-up approach includes entrepreneurship training and the promotion of community savings and credit schemes to build local financial autonomy.

As a cross-cutting priority, APSDI promotes digital transformation and innovation, modernising local trade and agriculture by training SMEs and cooperatives in sustainable digital tools. We integrate innovative technologies into our internal management and community services to bridge the rural digital divide.

Information, Knowledge Management & R&D Advisory Services

The program broadens APSDI’s knowledge base on peace, reconciliation and economic empowerment. By creating a platform for information sharing between the research community and policy decision-makers, it addresses major environmental and development issues through specialised workshops and seminars.

Building resilience requires partnership.

We welcome partnerships with donors, government institutions, UN agencies, civil society and the private sector to design and deliver programs that last across the Democratic Republic of Congo.
