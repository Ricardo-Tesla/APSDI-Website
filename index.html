Who We Are

Communities recovering. Resilience taking root.

We envision a future where communities throughout the Democratic Republic of Congo enjoy their human dignity and sustainable resilience.

APSDI drives development through an integrated approach — linking environmental protection, food security, public health and social protection with peacebuilding, and mainstreaming gender equality through every program.

Overall Objective

To enhance the socio-economic resilience of conflict-affected populations in the DRC by fostering peaceful coexistence, protecting natural resources, improving health outcomes, strengthening food security, and mainstreaming gender equality.

What We Stand For

Ten values guide every program.

They shape how staff engage communities, how partners are chosen, and how progress is measured.

Peace & Social Cohesion

Promoting dialogue, reconciliation and peaceful coexistence among communities.

Human Dignity & Human Rights

Respecting the rights and equal worth of every person, without discrimination.

Gender Equality & Social Inclusion

Equal opportunity and participation for women, men, youth and marginalised groups.

Community Ownership & Participation

Empowering communities to lead decision-making, planning and implementation.

Environmental Stewardship

Protecting natural resources and promoting climate and biodiversity resilience.

Integrity, Transparency & Accountability

Managing resources ethically and remaining accountable to communities and partners.

Resilience & Sustainable Development

Strengthening capacity to withstand shocks and sustain progress beyond a project's life.

Partnership & Collaboration

Working with government, civil society, UN agencies and the private sector.

Innovation & Learning

Promoting evidence-based programming and continuous, adaptive learning.

Equity & Justice

Ensuring fair access to services and opportunities for vulnerable populations.

Governance

Built on transparency and shared oversight.

Three statutory bodies keep APSDI accountable to the communities and partners it serves.

General Assembly

The organisation's members convene to set overall direction and provide strategic oversight.

Board of Directors

Guides strategy and decision-making between assembly sessions.

Supervisory Board

Responsible for administrative and financial oversight, ensuring transparency and accountability.

Building resilience requires partnership.

We welcome partnerships with donors, government institutions, UN agencies, civil society and the private sector to design and deliver programs that last across the Democratic Republic of Congo.
